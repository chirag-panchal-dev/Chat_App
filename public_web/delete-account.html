Delete your Hushlo account

In the app

  1. Open Hushlo and go to Settings.
  2. Choose "Delete account" and confirm. You may be asked to sign in again first.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, username, settings, device tokens, block list and profile photo.
  • Messages you sent and photos you uploaded in your conversations.
  • Your sign-in account.

Messages sent by the other person remain visible to them, and the conversation will show "Deleted account". Abuse reports you filed or that concern you may be retained for safety and legal reasons.
