1. What we collect
- Account data: email address (or Google/Apple sign-in identifier), display name, username, optional photo and short bio.
- Content: messages and photos you send, and the conversations they belong to.
- Settings: notification, preview and theme preferences; blocked accounts.
- Device data for notifications: a push token, platform (Android/iOS) and app version.
- Reports you submit about other accounts.
- Technical data processed by Firebase services (for example IP address and device/app diagnostics) as described in Google's Firebase privacy documentation.

2. How we use it
To provide messaging, deliver notifications, keep the service safe (block/report), and let you delete your account.
We do not sell your data and do not use message content for advertising.

3. Who can see what
- Other signed-in people can see your display name, username, photo and bio only if they know your exact username or are in a conversation with you.
- Messages are visible to the participants of the conversation.
- Reports are visible only to authorized moderators.

4. Security — important
Messages are encrypted in transit and stored with Google Firebase, protected by access rules. Messages are NOT end-to-end encrypted: the service provider's infrastructure can technically access stored data.

5. Notifications
Notification previews are off by default. When off, notifications say "New message" and contain no message text.

6. Retention and deletion
You can delete your account in Settings → Account → Delete account, or request deletion at the public deletion page. On deletion we remove your profile, username, settings, push tokens, uploaded photos and the messages you sent. 7. Your choices
You can edit your profile, change notification and preview settings, block and report accounts, and delete your account at any time.
