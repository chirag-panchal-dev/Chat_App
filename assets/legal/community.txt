Hushlo Community Guidelines (DRAFT)

- Be respectful. No harassment, hate, threats or bullying.
- No spam, scams or impersonation.
- No sexual content involving minors, no illegal content, no content that encourages self-harm or violence.
- Only share photos you have the right to share.
- Respect people's choice to stop talking: if someone blocks you, do not try to contact them another way.

Block someone from the chat menu. Report a person or a specific message from the long-press menu or chat menu. Reports are sent privately to our moderation team and are never shown to the other person. We review reports as capacity allows; we do not promise round-the-clock review.
